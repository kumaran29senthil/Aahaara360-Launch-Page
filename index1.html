A Complete Ecosystem for Modern Ayurveda

AI Prakriti Analysis

Understand your patient's core constitution with our data-driven 10-question quiz and 6 Rasa analysis.

The Architect AI

Instantly generate safe, rule-based 7-day diet plans based on Prakriti, Agni, and health goals.

The Specialist AI

Refine plans with intelligent food swaps powered by Vector Search and Cosine Similarity.

360° Patient Loop

Connect seamlessly with patients through the dedicated Flutter mobile app for plans and reminders.

AI Intelligence Tools

Leverage Gemini AI for a real-time Ayurvedic Chatbot, Food Scanner, and Recipe Finder.

Secure & Scalable

Built on a multi-tenant architecture with Supabase RLS, ready to onboard thousands of dietitians securely.

1

Profile & Assess Patient

2

Generate & Refine Plan

3

Engage The Patient
