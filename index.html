About

Local governments often face challenges in promptly resolving civic issues. Praja Shakthi bridges this gap with a crowdsourced solution where citizens can report problems, track updates, and receive transparent communication. On the backend, municipal staff manage, route, and resolve issues with real-time dashboards and analytics.
